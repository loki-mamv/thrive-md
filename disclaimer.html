Emergencies

In an emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number). For mental health crises in the US, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Do not wait on information from a website.

This is information, not advice

Everything on thrive.md and across the .md health network is general health information published for education. It is not medical advice. Reading it does not create a clinician-patient relationship with anyone involved in producing it.

General information cannot account for your medical history, your other conditions, the medications you take, your allergies, whether you are pregnant, or your age and kidney or liver function. Those are exactly the factors that determine whether something is right for you. Only a clinician who can assess you is in a position to make that call.

Always talk to a clinician first

  • Do not start, stop, or change the dose of any medication based on something you read here
  • Do not delay seeking care, or disregard advice you have already been given, because of something on this site
  • Do not use this site to self-diagnose a symptom that worries you
  • Bring what you read here to your clinician as a question, which is what it is good for

Dosing and drug information

Where we describe medications, we describe what appears on FDA-approved labeling and in published clinical guidelines. We do not tell individual readers what to take or how much. Drug information also changes: labels are revised, safety communications are issued, and guidelines are updated. Check with your prescriber or pharmacist.

AI-generated answers

The Q&A feature on these sites generates answers with an AI language model. It can be wrong, out of date, or missing something important about your situation, and it can be confidently wrong. Everything on this page applies to those answers at least as strongly as it applies to our written articles.

Accuracy and currency

We cite primary sources, review pages periodically, and correct errors when we find them or when a reader reports one. We cannot guarantee that any page reflects the very latest evidence at the moment you read it. Our editorial policy explains how content is produced and reviewed. If you spot something wrong, email corrections@thrive.md.

No endorsement

Mentioning a product, test, clinic, app, or organization is not an endorsement, and the absence of a mention is not a judgment against it.
